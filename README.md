# Blinkit Sales Performance & Inventory Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive single-page Power BI report analyzing retail sales, customer ratings, product mix, and outlet performance across **8,500+ items** generating **$1.20M in Total Sales**.

## Key Features
- **Dynamic Metric Switching:** Built with **Field Parameters** to toggle all visuals seamlessly across *Total Sales*, *Average Sales*, *Average Rating*, and *Item Count*.
- **Data Preparation & Modeling:** Standardized categorical attributes in **Power Query** and engineered custom **DAX** measures for KPI tracking.
- **Custom Branded UI:** Single-view executive layout styled in Blinkit's brand palette for zero-scroll readability.

## Key Business Insights
- **Fat Content Impact:** Low Fat products generate **64.6% ($776.3K)** of total revenue compared to Regular items ($425.4K).
- **Top Product Categories:** *Fruits and Vegetables* (**$178.12K**) and *Snack Foods* (**$175.43K**) lead overall sales.
- **Outlet & Location Performance:** *Tier 3* locations drive the highest sales (**$472K**), with *Supermarket Type1* contributing **$787.55K** in total revenue.
